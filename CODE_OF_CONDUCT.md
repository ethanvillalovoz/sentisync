# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

SentiSync welcomes constructive issues, pull requests, and technical discussion. Please be respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of encouraged behavior:

- Share clear reproduction steps and context.
- Give feedback on code, docs, or design decisions without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about problems.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic comments.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that do not follow this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may lead to being blocked from participation.
